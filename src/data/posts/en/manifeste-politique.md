# *Manifesto*

## ***Preamble***

#### **A simple observation, a single reality:**

We love France and the universal values it promotes throughout the world. However, our country finds itself fractured and divided as a result of numerous disastrous policies implemented against the French people. The French refuse to see their ***culture***, their ***history***\**,** their ***way of life*** and everything that makes up the ***greatness of their country*** fade away. We must therefore restore to France what it deserves:

### **A place where everyone is respected, and a prosperous future.**

Our new political party, “Réveil Français”, therefore appears to be the only one capable of safeguarding France. We will introduce a **radical new policy,**

### **not based on senior civil servants but on what the French people ACTUALLY want.**

France will not recover through empty promises or slogans, but through the commitment of those who ***bring it to life every day.*** Politics must, first and foremost, address the concerns of the French people: their daily lives, their work, making ends meet, and their need for security and stability.

### **Our candidate, Alix LACROIX, pledges to implement a policy focused on the French people.**

A young woman from a working-class background and a modest family in Auvergne, she grew up watching her parents work hard day in, day out, only to have their money ***stolen*** at the end of the month. Today, she has become the director of a small business with 32 employees, specialising in timber framing and roofing.

She therefore understands the tough reality faced by businesses, which are ***strangled by unnecessary costs and regulations.***

These regulations prevent our French businesses from developing properly and from being ***competitive***.

### **We are going to repeal these laws so that employees’ lives can improve.**

We also believe in France’s ***economic renewal***, in particular through the country’s ***re-industrialisation***. We reaffirm our commitment to ***democracy*** and ***freedom***, which are the foundations of our country. Change will not happen on its own; it will come about through **the awakening of the French people**.

Our political commitment is simple: we fight for ***the people***, not for ***the elites.***

### **Let us be ready to give France and its people back what they deserve.**

## *We DEFEND*

### **La Nation:**

We see the nation as a community with a shared destiny, founded on a shared history, culture and values that unite citizens beyond their individual differences. It is this sense of shared belonging that fosters solidarity between generations and regions.

##### **We wish to reaffirm our national pride and the passing on of our shared heritage.**

### **State Authority:**

We advocate for the restoration of state authority, which alone can guarantee public order and equality for all before the law. Such a respected law enforcement body is not synonymous with authoritarianism, but rather with the ability to enforce its decisions without weakness or compromise.

##### **We want to restore to our institutions, our justice system and our law enforcement agencies the means and the legitimacy to act to the full.**

### **Heritage:**

We protect our heritage, whether it be tangible – in the form of our monuments and landscapes – or intangible – in the form of our traditions and collective memory. This heritage is the result of the hard work and sacrifices of the generations that came before us, and we have a duty to preserve it and pass it on.

##### **We want to protect what gives our country its soul in the face of standardisation and oblivion.**

### **Sovereignty:**

We want to preserve France’s sovereignty – that is to say, its ability to determine its own destiny, without being subject to excessive constraints from supranational bodies or foreign powers. This applies to our borders, our currency, our justice system and our strategic economic choices.

##### **A sovereign nation is a nation free to make its own political choices.**

### **Labour and Industry:**

We regard the value of work as the foundation of individual dignity and collective prosperity. We must therefore rebuild a strong industrial base here at home. Manufacturing in France means safeguarding jobs, expertise and strategic independence for the future.

##### **We want to encourage hard work, productive investment and recognition of manual workers.**

### **Businesses:**

We stand up for our businesses, our SMEs and our craftspeople, who are all too often stifled by excessive taxation and bureaucracy that hinders investment and job creation. A simplified tax system would help to unleash economic initiative, reward risk-taking and hard work, and give business leaders the means to hire staff and innovate. We want a state that supports our entrepreneurs rather than penalising them, so that economic success benefits everyone.

## *We are FIGHTING*

### **The Feeling of Abandonment:**

We are combating the sense of abandonment felt by many people living in rural and peri-urban areas, who are faced with the gradual closure of public services (hospitals, schools, shops) and their distance from decision-making centres. This feeling fuels a legitimate mistrust of political elites who are perceived as out of touch with everyday life.

##### **We want to reinvest in this region to restore genuine equality amongst all citizens.**

### **Impunity and Insecurity:**

In our view, impunity undermines public confidence in the justice system and institutions when penalties that are too lenient or rarely enforced no longer act as a deterrent to crime. The lack of safety in everyday life – on public transport, in shops and in neighbourhoods – restricts our fellow citizens’ genuine freedom to live in peace.

##### **We want a tougher justice system and a stronger police presence on the ground.**

### **Illegal Immigration:**

We are combating illegal immigration, which is beyond the state’s control and undermines its ability to organise reception, accommodation and integration under appropriate conditions. It fuels people-smuggling networks that exploit human distress and places increasing pressure on public services that are already stretched to breaking point (health, housing, education).

##### **We advocate for controlled immigration, determined by the nation itself.**

### **Unemployment**

The mass unemployment that has plagued our country for decades is depriving millions of French people of the dignity that comes with work, whilst placing a heavy burden on our public finances. It affects young people, older people and regions already weakened by deindustrialisation in particular, widening a social and regional divide that is becoming increasingly difficult to bridge.

We want an ambitious employment policy, based on training, making it easier to recruit staff and supporting the real economy, so that everyone can find their place in society through work.

### **Deindustrialisation:**

The deindustrialisation of our country has led to the loss of hundreds of thousands of industrial jobs over several decades, with production shifting to low-cost countries. This loss of expertise undermines our manufacturing sovereignty and our ability to produce what we need ourselves, particularly in times of crisis.

##### **We want to establish a strategic production facility in our region.**

### **Economic Dependence:**

We believe that France is overly dependent on other countries for energy, raw materials and critical technologies. This dependence makes us vulnerable to international crises and weakens our political decision-making capacity.

##### **We advocate for a high degree of national and European autonomy.**

## *Our Commitments*

### **We are committed to**

to serve France loyally, and to respect the law and the institutions of the Republic.

### **We are committed to**

to defend our ideas in democratic debate, through persuasion and by voting.

### **We call on**

all citizens who care deeply about their country to join us in reviving France.
